# Stepz — Delete your account and data

**App:** Stepz · **Developer:** Qudisoft · **Contact:** stepz.qdsoft@gmail.com

## How to delete your account from the app

1. Open **Stepz** and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Delete forever**.

Your account and all data stored on our server are deleted immediately.
The app signs you out on this device; you can keep using Stepz without an account.

## How to request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to
**stepz.qdsoft@gmail.com** from the Google account email you used to sign in,
with the subject "Delete my Stepz account". We will delete your account
within **30 days** and confirm by email.
